## 0. Facts the plan rests on (verified on this machine, Apple M4, Python 3.13.5)

* Tests at the start of V4-final: 53 pass, 2 skip (`.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests -q`, ~60 s).
* Task 1 architecture (code is authoritative, see `src/racestate.py`, `src/strategy.py::_phase1_race_state`, `src/live/engine.py::_race_state_extra`): pre-race, every plan **group** (start compound, second compound, stop count) is solved as a *symmetric* pack of four rivals on the same plan (`pack_equilibrium`); the group's term `V (places(s) − places(s*))` is charged on the first stop; later stops keep V3's undercut exposure at `undercut_lambda`; the first-stop history prior is off (`kappa = 0`) whenever the race state is on. Live, each option's next stop is re-priced against the four nearest rivals in virtual position (`relevant_rivals`, `live_position_term`) and V3's density traffic is replaced by `rejoin_traffic` from the screen.
* Constants (`RaceStateConstants`, leave-one-out over the other 2026 races): place gap `V` = median interval between adjacent classified finishers at the last lap both completed (4.6–5.9 s, n = 103–120 gaps); persistence `ψ` = share of adjacent first-pit-cycle pairs (first stops within 5 laps, adjacent positions the lap before the earlier stop) whose order 2 laps after the later stop is the order at the flag (**25–35 pairs**, 0.68–0.81); `σ_rel = √2 × 1.4826 × MAD` of green pit loss (2.5–2.9 s); pack gaps = intervals on laps 5–15 at P2–P15.
* Calibration (`data/processed/calibration.json`, V3, LOO per weekend): `undercut_lambda` 0.2 (0.0 for the Barcelona fold), `plan_prior_tau_s` 4.0, `first_stop_kappa_s` 1.0 (0.0 for Barcelona and Hungary folds), grid penalty 0.0, manage cost 0.6 / floor 0.45. **The sweeps were run without the race state** (`scripts/80_recalibrate.py::Donor.search` never passes `race_state`). Under V4 `kappa` is structurally 0 and `lambda` only prices stops after the first, so the first-stop objective no longer identifies `lambda`.
* Production paths still on V3's objective: `strategy.counterfactual` as called from `scripts/10_pipeline.py` (kappa = `cal.first_stop_kappa_s`, no race-state term), `src/outlook.py::sim_kwargs/eval_kwargs` (kappa on, no race state), `app/desk_tab.py::_evaluate/_card_numbers` (`evaluate_plans` with no position terms at all), `strategy.evaluate_plans` and `strategy.deg_crossover` (no race-state parameter). `bench_outlook` inherits the outlook's objective.
* **Belgium gate** (`every recommended stint length is one the compound was run to`): the shipped plan is 1-stop M-S @ 16 → SOFT for 28 laps; the field ran M-H (18 of 19 one-stopped, modal M-H, tool's sequence share 0.05). The SOFT stint cap is 28 = `SUPPORT_EXTRAPOLATION_LIMIT (2.0) × practice support (14 laps)`; the circuit history cap is 32 (longest 29, from **3** SOFT stints in 2024); the practice fit's SOFT rate (0.158 s/lap) is *below* MEDIUM's (0.179) and HARD's (0.173), so the model's uncapped SOFT life is 38.6 laps and the stint pays no cliff cost. Race-measured: SOFT median stint 12, max 26 (63 % of first stops under SC). V3's later first stop (18) gave a 26-lap SOFT stint and passed. **The cause is compound feasibility under extrapolation: a stint planned at the edge of the practice support on a compound whose rate is poorly identified is priced as if the extrapolation were certain.** The fix (WP-B) is a tyre-life uncertainty mechanism that grows with the extrapolation beyond support, measured on the seven weekends, not a Belgian cap.
* Haas: `#31 OCO`, `#87 BEA`, team `Haas F1 Team` in every 2026 lap table. Practice long-run evidence per weekend is thin (Hungary: OCO 9 clean MEDIUM laps + 3 SOFT, BEA 8 MEDIUM). The posterior carries `dev[d, c]` for 20 drivers; `percar.team_pooled_dev` already pools driver→team. Calibration `team_factors["Haas F1 Team"] = 1.19`, driver factors OCO 1.02–1.20, BEA 1.20–1.39 across folds (from previous races, shrunk by `ln_sd` ≈ 0.08–0.10). No sector times in the lap tables (`src/ingest.py` carries none); FastF1's cache has them.
* Plan priors available pre-race (`fitstage_*.json["plan_prior"]`, mapped through Pirelli nominations): sequences, starts, stops with counts; `circuit_history.first_stop_green` has `by_compound`, `by_stops`, `by_compound_stops` cells (Barcelona: M|2 16, M|3 14, H|2 5; Hungary: M|2 27, S|2 10, M|1 9 …). Barcelona's mapped history has **no SOFT starts** (2023/25 C3 = 2026 MEDIUM) while 5 of 13 2026 finishers started SOFT: a rival start-compound model that only reads history cannot produce Barcelona's first movers; one that reads the *model's own family costs* can.
* Live replays: `data/raw/livetiming/2026_hungary_race`, `2026_barcelona_race` (Task 1 tick medians on the slow laptop 298/341 ms; V3 on this Mac ≈ 75 ms). Budget: **< 200 ms per tick here**.
* Benchmark suite: `bench/run_all.sh` (accuracy, strategy, ablation, outlook, live, stability, speed, apex, pytest, compare; 24 min on this machine for Task 1) then `bench/bench_live.py --no-race-state`, `bench/bench_tick_paired.py`, `bench/bench_v4_compare.py`. The decide stage per weekend is 37–55 s; `80_recalibrate.py` 366 s (V3 objective).

## 1. Architecture (the layers every package must respect)

```
telemetry/data → tyre state (src/tyre.py, src/cliff.py)
              → Haas car state (src/haascar.py, src/percar.py)               [WP-E]
              → race state (src/racestate.py: constants, rival field, live rivals)  [WP-A, WP-D]
              → candidate actions (strategy families: src/strategy.py; live options: src/live/engine.py)
              → expected race outcomes (one objective: src/objective.py)      [WP-F]
              → PIT / STAY / WINDOW recommendation (racestate.action_table + live decision)  [WP-D]
              → explanation (src/explain.py)                                  [WP-D, WP-E]
              → presentation (app/)                                           [WP-G]
```

* **Strategy-family layer** answers *what broad plan is plausible*: compound nomination, the plan-family prior `τ`, tyre life (uncertainty-aware, WP-B), race distance, stint caps. It never chooses the lap.
* **Race-execution layer** answers *when to execute the next stop*: position, nearby competitors (rival field pre-race, real rivals live), tyre state, traffic, undercut/overcut, rejoin, SC/VSC. The first-stop history prior stays off everywhere in production (`kappa = 0` under V4); history enters the execution layer only as the *rivals'* plausible behaviour (WP-A), never as a term on our own lap.
* Strategy scoring lives in `src/strategy.py` (`simulate_model`, `pit_window_model`, `evaluate_plans`, `counterfactual`) and `src/live/engine.py` (`_plan_prepare/_plan_finish`); nothing else may re-implement a cost. The objective's *parameters* are assembled once by `src/objective.py::V4Objective` and splatted into those functions.

### WP-A — Heterogeneous rival field and the place-value estimator (`src/racestate.py`, `src/strategy.py::_phase1_race_state`)

**A1. Leave-one-out with a set.** `measure_constants(exclude: str | Iterable[str] | None = None, donors=DONOR_EVENTS, estimator: str = "regularized")`. A string or any iterable of keys is excluded (the recalibration needs to exclude both the held-out weekend and the donor being searched).

**A2. Place-value estimator audit and regularisation.** Keep the Task 1 measurement code and add, per donor race, the raw counts (`pairs`, `kept`, finishing gaps, lead-lap gaps, gaps by finishing-position band). `RaceStateConstants` gains: `estimator`, `persistence_raw` (pooled kept/pairs), `persistence_by_donor` (dict), `place_value_sd_s`, `place_value_ci_s` (5–95 %, bootstrap over donor races and pairs), `place_gap_midfield_s` (diagnostic: gaps between finishers P7–P16), `n_cycle_pairs_by_donor`. Estimators, selectable and all reported by `bench/bench_place_value.py` (new; writes `bench/out/place_value.json` with per-fold n, ψ, V, CI, and sensitivity to: lapped cars (lead-lap vs all classified), position band, SC vs green races, cycle-pair window 3/5/8 laps, adjacency 1 vs ≤2):
  * `task1` — exactly Task 1 (all classified finishers, pooled ratio).
  * `lead_lap` — V from lead-lap finishers.
  * `all_classified` — same as task1 (kept as its own name for the report).
  * `regularized` — **the V4 production estimator, chosen before any benchmark is run**: ψ by empirical-Bayes shrinkage of the per-race ratios toward the pooled value (beta-binomial: method-of-moments between-race variance; if the between-race variance estimate is ≤ 0 the pooled ratio with a Jeffreys Beta(½,½) prior is used), V as the pooled median of all-classified finishing gaps with a bootstrap CI; `place_value_s = V (2ψ − 1)` at the shrunk ψ; `place_value_sd_s` from the delta method / bootstrap. The estimator is chosen on statistical grounds (it is the standard treatment of a small binomial sample pooled over heterogeneous races) — **not** by benchmark score. The four are compared in E2.

**A3. Rival field (replaces the symmetric pack as the default).** New `RivalFieldConfig` dataclass: `mode: "hetero" | "symmetric"`, `family_temper_s: float` (default 3.0 s until calibrated; WP-F calibrates), `history_weight_k0: float = 10.0` (pseudo-count; the historical first-stop prior's weight is `n_cell / (n_cell + k0)`), `use_history_prior: bool = True`, `rate_levels: int = 3`, `rate_ln_sd: float | None` (None → sd of the log LOO team factors from the calibration, fallback 0.12), `family_prior_weight_k0: float = 10.0`.
  * A rival **type** = (plan group g, degradation level r). Groups are the same `(start, second, n_stops)` groups `_phase1_race_state` already builds curves for; levels are 3 lognormal quantiles (−1, 0, +1 sd) of a rate factor applied to the *first stint's* cost (`stay_cum`) and the fresh-set cost of that type (approximate `T_g^r(l) = T_g(l) + (stay_cum^r[l] − stay_cum[l])`; document the approximation).
  * Type weight = `q_f(g) × q_r`, with `q_f(g) ∝ exp(−C_g / family_temper_s) × p_hist(g)^{w_f}` where `C_g` is the group's best *tyre + pit + traffic + SC* cost (no priors), `p_hist(g)` the smoothed plan-prior frequency (`plan_prior_penalty(seq, prior, tau=1)` gives `−log p`), `w_f = n / (n + family_prior_weight_k0)`; the start-compound marginal and stop-count marginal back it off exactly as `plan_prior_penalty` does. A family with no plan prior (new circuit) uses the season pool the pipeline already passes.
  * Each type's stop-lap choice: `q_t(l) ∝ exp(−(T_t(l) + V·places_t(l)) / CHOICE_TEMPER_S) × p_hist(l | start, stops)^{w_l}` with `p_hist` from the `first_stop_prior` tables the pipeline already passes (`exp(−neglogp)`) and `w_l = n_cell / (n_cell + history_weight_k0)`; `use_history_prior=False` sets `w_l = 0`.
  * **Mean-field equilibrium over types** (exact expectation over independently sampled rivals, not Monte Carlo): every type faces the type-weighted field on the four pack slots (two ahead, two behind at the measured pack-gap quantiles, as `pack_slots`); iterate damped as `pack_equilibrium` does until the type choice distributions converge. Then, for each of **our** groups, our term is the best response to the converged field with the cover response on (`expected_ahead` with cover, vectorised over types). Precompute the pairwise `D` tensors once per search; the per-group cost must stay well under 0.5 s at 500 draws (per-car plans call this 20+ times).
  * `pack_equilibrium` stays for `mode="symmetric"` and must reproduce Task 1 bit for bit (`no_race_state` / symmetric ablations depend on it).
  * `res.race_state` gains `mode`, `rival_field` (type table: family, rate level, weight, stop median/IQR, `p_stop_by_lap` summary) and, per group, the `pack` dict in the same shape as before (`laps, tyre_s, places, position_s, cost_s, term_s, q, best_lap, tyre_best_lap, q_median, q_p25_p75, converged`), so `scripts/10_pipeline.py::race_state_block`, `40_weekend.py`, `bench_ablation.py` and the tests keep working.
  * `simulate_model(..., race_state=const, race_state_cover=True, rival_field: RivalFieldConfig | None = None)`: `rival_field=None` with a race state means the **hetero default**; `RivalFieldConfig(mode="symmetric")` is Task 1. The same keyword goes through `simulate`, `search_with_pace_calibration`, `per_driver_plans`.
  * Tests (mechanism): a SOFT-start type stops earlier than a MEDIUM-start type on the same tyre model; raising `family_temper_s` flattens the family distribution; `use_history_prior=False` changes only the rivals' stop distributions; symmetric mode equals Task 1's `pack_equilibrium` result on the synthetic pack; the constants with a set exclusion equal the constants with the single key when the set has one element; the regularised ψ lies between the pooled ratio and the prior and its CI contains the point estimate.

### WP-B — Uncertainty-aware tyre life (`src/tyre.py`, and the callers that build models)

**B1. Measurement first.** `bench/bench_extrapolation.py` (new, offline, writes `bench/out/extrapolation.json`): over the seven weekends, from `scorestint_*.parquet` / the sealed-vs-race stint scores, the practice→race rate error (log ratio of race-measured stint rate to the sealed prediction) as a function of the stint length relative to the practice support (`fitstage_*.json["age_support_by_compound"]`), in bins (≤ 1×, 1–1.5×, 1.5–2×). Report n, bias and dispersion (robust sd) per bin and the pooled regime `ln_sd`. If dispersion grows with extrapolation, the scale below is the measured slope; if it does not, the scale is the pooled regime `ln_sd` and the report says so.

**B2. Mechanism.** `TyreModel` gains `support: dict` (compound → practice age support, laps) and `extrap_ln_sd: float` (log-sd of the wear-rate uncertainty at 2× the support, linearly growing from 0 at 1× support; source: B1). `cost_table` prices a stint of length `L` on compound `c` with the per-draw rate multiplied by `exp(z_d · s(L))`, `s(L) = extrap_ln_sd · max(0, L/support_c − 1)`, `z_d` a fixed standard-normal draw paired with the posterior draw (seeded, so the tables are deterministic) — so the expected cost carries a risk premium *only* through the cliff's convexity, and only beyond the support. `wear_at`, `life_table` and `life_laps` report the same widening (`life_lo/life_hi` widen with extrapolation). New `TyreModel.life_risk(compound, L, start, push, event) → {p_cliff, expected_excess_s, life_p10/p50/p90}` for the action tables and the UI. `copy_with`/`subsample`/`for_driver` carry the new fields. Default `extrap_ln_sd = 0.0` keeps every existing number bit for bit; the pipeline, the weekend script, the outlook and the live `WeekendModel` set it from the measured value (a module constant `EXTRAP_LN_SD_MEASURED` in `src/tyre.py` with its source comment, overridable by `calibration.json["extrap_ln_sd"]` if WP-F writes one).
  * Do **not** change `life_caps` semantics or `SUPPORT_EXTRAPOLATION_LIMIT`; the caps stay as search bounds. Do not change the fit.
  * `strategy.compound_life`/`life_table` must state life as a range (`life_lo`, `life_hi`) that widens beyond the support; the report needs "28 ± k".
  * Regression test for the Belgium failure (generic): a synthetic model whose compound X has a long model life but a 14-lap support; with `extrap_ln_sd=0` a 28-lap stint on X costs the same as the point estimate, with the measured value it carries a premium that grows with `L/support`, and a stint at 1× support carries none. Plus: on the real Belgium posterior, the recommended plan's stints are all inside the observed range **or** the report states they are not (the test asserts the premium exists, not the benchmark outcome).

### WP-C — Position-aware strategy metric and Haas benchmark rows (`bench/`)

**C1. Definition (fixed before any V4 benchmark runs; write it into `docs/v4_methodology.md`).** For a candidate plan `p` with first stop `s_p`, on the weekend's **oracle** tyre model (race-measured rates, as `bench_strategy.oracle_model`):

```
J(p)      = T_oracle(p) + V · L(p)
L(p)      = mean over the field's green first stops l_r (classified finishers, in-laps not under SC)
            of  Σ_slots [ Φ((g_slot + D(s_p, l_r)) / σ_rel) − Φ(g_slot / σ_rel) ]
D(s, l)   = A_me(s) − A_r(l): our race time minus the rival's from lap 0 to max(s, l)+1,
            each on its own set (ours: p's start compound then p's second; the rival's: the
            same start compound as p and the modal second compound of the field's plans
            with that start), priced on the oracle model at push 1, pit loss included
g_slot    = the four pack slots (two ahead, two behind) at the measured pack-gap quantiles
V, σ_rel  = the weekend's leave-one-out race-state constants (regularised estimator)
R_pos(p)  = J(p) − min_{q ∈ C} J(q),   C = { p's family with the first stop on every legal lap,
            later stops re-optimised on the oracle } ∪ { oracle_opt, field_modal, winner, tyre_optimal }
```

`L(p)` is the expected number of places lost through the first pit cycle against the field as it actually stopped (negative = gained), `V·L` its value in race-time seconds, and `R_pos` the position-aware regret. Report per weekend and pooled: `R_pos(tool)`, `R_pos(tyre_optimal)`, `R_pos(field_modal)`, `L(tool)`, `L(field_modal)`, and `P_retain(tool) = 1 − L(tool)/4` clipped to [0, 1] as the retention probability. **Keep the pure-time oracle regret unchanged** (same code path, same keys).

**C2. Haas rows.** In `bench_strategy.py`: per weekend, for OCO and BEA, the per-car plan's first stop vs the driver's actual green first stop, whether the per-car plan shares the field plan's shape, and the per-car `L`. In `bench_live.py`: the per-stop rows already carry `driver`; add `stops_haas` (share within 3 laps, median |error|, box-now cost) for OCO/BEA, and a `decision_stability` block: share of car-laps where the plan's `decision.action` changed from the previous lap without a material state change (a pit stop by the car or a relevant rival, an SC/VSC change, or a ≥ 1 s change in the best-vs-runner-up margin). Read `plan["decision"]` defensively (Task 1 plans have none).

**C3. `bench/bench_v4_compare.py`** compares **V4 final (bench/out) against Task 1 (bench/v4_task1)** and V3 (bench/v3): add the position-aware rows, the Haas rows, the estimator table from `place_value.json`, and the E0–E8 experiment table from `bench/out/experiments.json` (WP-F/integration writes it). Keep the V3 columns.

### WP-D — Live race-execution engine (`src/live/engine.py`, new `src/live/rivals.py`, new `src/explain.py`)

* **Competitor selection** (`rivals.py::strategic_rivals`): the cars that can exchange a place with us through the next pit cycle: within `RELEVANT_RANGE_S` in virtual position (as now) **plus** the car directly ahead and directly behind on track whatever the gap, minus lapped cars (a lap down) and cars whose remaining stops make interaction impossible; capped at `k` by strategic relevance (|virtual gap| / σ_rel, then closeness). Report why each rival is in the set. Investigate k = 3/4/5/6 on the two replays for accuracy and tick time and pick the smallest k that is not worse (document the numbers).
* **Rejoin and traffic projection**: project every car's race time forward to the candidate rejoin lap using its current stint pace (fuel-corrected recent laps) and its expected stop (the plan the engine gave it last lap), instead of today's gaps; the rejoin position and the cars within the traffic band come from that projection (`rivals.py::project_rejoin`). Keep `racestate.rejoin_traffic` as the fallback when a car has no pace estimate.
* **Pit-response**: keep `expected_ahead`'s cover; add to each rival's detail the probability it covers if we pit now, and the outcome if it does (`if_cover`).
* **Decision and stability**: `plan["decision"] = {action ∈ {"PIT NOW", "STAY OUT", "WAIT k LAPS", "BOX BY LAP x"}, wait_laps, box_by_lap, confidence, projected_position, delta_vs_alternative_s, rivals: [...], if_cover, life: {p_cliff_before_stop, life_p10/p50/p90}, reasons: [...], held_since_lap, changed}`. Confidence = share of the car's posterior draws on which the chosen action's cost is the lowest of the five (the shortlist already prices options draw by draw). Hysteresis: the previous lap's action is kept unless the new best beats it by `DECISION_HYSTERESIS_S` (engineering constant, 0.3 s = V3's `BOX_NOW_TOL_S` order; sensitivity on the replays) or the state changed materially (our stop, a rival's stop, SC/VSC, the window closing).
* **Explanations** (`src/explain.py`, pure functions from model state, used by the live engine and by the pre-race decision table): produce the principal reason and 2–4 supporting reasons from the action table parts (tyre, position, traffic), the rival list, the rejoin projection and the life risk, e.g. "Staying out 2 laps costs 1.4 s of tyre; RUS behind enters its window; pitting now retains P15 with 78 % confidence". Never hard-code a strategy sentence that is not derived from a number in the state.
* **Tyre-life risk in the action table**: `p_cliff_before_stop` per action from the car's `laps_to_cliff` posterior; shown, not decisive.
* Tick budget: measure with `bench/bench_tick_paired.py` on both replays; stay < 200 ms median on this machine; do not add cars or laps of projection without a measured accuracy gain (`bench_live.py`).
* Tests: rival selection includes the car directly behind at 8 s and excludes a lapped car; the projection moves a car that is stopping; hysteresis holds a decision within the margin and releases on a rival's stop; the decision dict serialises; the V3 path (`race_state=False`) is unchanged.

### WP-F — One objective (`src/objective.py`, outlook, desk, counterfactual, recalibration)

* `V4Objective` dataclass: `race_state: RaceStateConstants | None`, `rival_field: RivalFieldConfig`, `undercut_lambda` (later stops only), `plan_prior`, `plan_prior_tau_s`, `first_stop_kappa_s = 0.0` (V3 only; asserting 0 in production), `traffic_s_per_lap`, `grid_penalty_s`, `sc_rate`, `extrap_ln_sd`. Builders: `V4Objective.for_event(ev, cal, fs/meta, ...)`, `.sim_kwargs()`, `.window_kwargs(res)`, `.eval_kwargs(res)`, `.counterfactual_kwargs(res)`; a `label` ("V4 objective: race state (hetero rival field), λ on later stops, τ plan prior, no first-stop prior").
* `strategy.evaluate_plans`, `strategy.deg_crossover`, `strategy.counterfactual` accept `race_state_terms: dict[group_label → (n_laps+1,) array]` (from `res.race_state["packs"]` via `racestate.term_by_lap`) and charge the plan's group term on its first stop in place of the first stop's undercut exposure, exactly as `pit_window_model` does; a plan whose group has no term is charged nothing and flagged `race_state: "no term for this family"`.

## 4. Benchmark procedure (final)

1. `pytest tests -q` green.
2. `scripts/80_recalibrate.py` (V4 objective) → `calibration.json`; then `10_pipeline.py --stage decide` on the seven weekends (fits untouched); `40_weekend.py`/outlook for the live weekends as the repo's commands require.

4. Any code fix after step 3 → repeat from step 2. The report quotes only the last run.
5. `results_v4.md` → `results_v4.pdf` (`bench/md2pdf.py`).
